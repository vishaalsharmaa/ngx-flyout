What is a flyout?

A flyout is a panel that slides in from the edge of the screen. Think of the notification panel on your phone, or the "details" panel that opens when you click a row in an email app or a file manager.

ngx-flyout gives you exactly that as one Angular component. It covers the full height of the screen on the right and dims the page behind it, and it can slide in if you like. On phones it fills the whole screen.

Why your product should have a flyout

Every time you send someone to a new page or throw a popup in their face, they lose their place. A flyout lets them do a quick job next to what they were looking at, then carry on.

People keep their place

The page stays in the background. Nobody has to scroll back to where they were or wonder which row they clicked.

Fewer interruptions

A popup says "stop and answer me". A flyout says "here's some help, take your time". It feels like part of the page, not a roadblock.

Room for real content

It is as tall as the screen and scrolls, so a full form, a list of details or a set of settings fits without cramming.

Faster everyday tasks

Quick views, quick edits and quick adds take one click to open and one click to close. No page loads, no lost filters, no back button.

Use cases

Quick view of a table row
Click an order, invoice or user and see the key details without opening a full page.
Edit or quick-add forms
Edit a record or add a new one while the list stays visible.
Filters and view settings
Pick filters or table columns and watch the page update behind the panel.
Notifications and activity
A scrollable list that can grow without taking over the page.
Help and details
Explain a feature or show more information next to where it is used.

Why ngx-flyout?

You could build a side panel yourself. Here is what you get without writing any of it:

  • Zero dependencies. Only Angular itself. No Angular CDK, no Material, no extra CSS file.
  • Built on the browser's own <dialog>. It always shows on top of everything, even from inside a small or scrolling component. No z-index fights.
  • Accessible out of the box. Keyboard focus moves in and comes back, Escape closes it, and screen readers announce the heading.
  • Small. Around 5 kB gzipped, and removed from your bundle if you don't use it.
  • Modern Angular. Standalone component, signals, [(opened)] two-way binding. Works with Angular 19, 20 and 21.
  • Easy to theme. Change the colour, width or speed with a CSS variable. It uses your app's font.
  • Safe for server-side rendering. Browser-only code runs only in the browser.

Getting started

Three steps, about two minutes.

1. Install

2. Import it in your component

3. Add a button and a flyout

That's it. Clicking the button opens the flyout; Cancel, ×, Escape or OK close it. Add animation="slide" to make it slide in and out. Want more? See the examples.

Opening and closing

Pick whichever fits your code. They all stay in sync.

Template reference (simplest)

Two-way binding

When the open state lives in your component. Use [(opened)], not one-way [opened], so your value updates when the user closes the flyout.

From TypeScript

For example, close it after a save finishes.

open(), close() and toggle() never fire (confirm) or (cancel). Those only fire when the user clicks or presses a key.

Methods

  • open(): opens the flyout.
  • close(): closes it.
  • toggle(): opens it if closed, closes it if open.

Content slots

  • Default: anything you put inside <ngx-flyout> becomes the body.
  • ngxFlyoutIcon: an icon shown next to the heading (import NgxFlyoutIcon).
  • ngxFlyoutFooter: your own footer instead of Cancel / OK (import NgxFlyoutFooter).

Dark mode

Set the background and text colour; the greys and lines follow.

App-wide defaults

Optional. Change the defaults for every flyout in one place, for example to translate the button text. Anything you set on a single flyout still wins.

Accessibility

  • Opens as a real modal dialog: the page behind can't be clicked or tabbed into.
  • Focus moves into the flyout when it opens and returns to the button that opened it.
  • Escape closes it, and screen readers read the heading and sub-heading.
  • The × button has a label ("Close") you can translate.
  • All buttons are at least 44px tall, easy to hit on touch screens.
  • The slide animation is off by default, and skipped for people who ask their device for reduced motion.

Browser support

Works in all current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. The optional slide animation uses newer CSS; in a browser without it, the flyout simply appears without sliding. Firefox slides it in but closes it at once.

The page is still there behind this panel. Close it with:

  • the Got it or Cancel button
  • the × in the corner
  • the Escape key

Everything inside this panel is your own content, passed in with ng-content.
